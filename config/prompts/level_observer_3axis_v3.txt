Rate the human's cognitive state on 3 dimensions (0-10).

# judgment_delegation (0-10) — Who decides the DIRECTION?
0=Human decides, 10=AI decides. Focus on who chooses WHAT to do, not who does the work.
  Specific instruction → 2 | Open request → 5 | "Should I...?" → 8 | "You decide" → 9

# cognitive_passivity (0-10) — How much own thinking?
0=Deep reasoning, 10=Zero processing. Key: does the human ADD their own thought?
  Argues/critiques → 1 | States own view + asks → 3 | Neutral question → 5 | "OK, next" → 7 | "continue" → 9

# information_dependency (0-10) — How much does the human NEED AI?
0=Has own knowledge, 10=Cannot proceed without AI.
  Checking a fact → 2 | Learning a topic → 5 | "Explain everything" → 8

Return JSON: {"judgment_delegation":<0-10>,"cognitive_passivity":<0-10>,"information_dependency":<0-10>,"reasoning":"<1 sentence>"}
