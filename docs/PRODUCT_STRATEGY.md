# Product Strategy: Agent–Agent Judgment and Critical-Thinking Measurement

## Decision

JDVP measures how interacting agents form, delegate, verify, challenge, and revise judgments. An agent may be Human or AI: Agent–Agent is the umbrella, not a synonym for AI–AI. Human–Human, Human–AI, and AI–AI are relationship profiles within that scope. Multi-party graphs are a later structural extension.

The two primary deliverables are a low-cost detector and a critical-thinking measurement framework. Develop them together through an automated research loop: sample evidence, obtain high-capability AI judgments, challenge the rubric, distill the detector, evaluate on untouched data, and promote or retain versions. Human experts are not a required labeling, adjudication, or release stage.

This is a development assumption that capable reference models can perform those tasks; it is not a claim that a named model has achieved AGI. Reference quality must still be tested. External behavior and outcomes absent from a conversation are checked through sampling of authorized records, tool traces, tests, and outcomes rather than a mandatory human-expert panel.

## Common Core, Relationship Profiles, Applications

| Layer | Purpose | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common measurement core | Authority movement, evidence, verification, alternatives, challenge, revision, and temporal change | Research scope; current four axes are hypotheses, not immutable universal dimensions |
| Human–AI profile | Critical-thinking practice and deliberate delegation in AI collaboration | Canonical v1.5 implementation available |
| Human–Human profile | Evidence isolation, alternatives being closed off, asymmetric authority, repeated judgment invalidation | Proposed; application candidates include scam, coercive/cult recruitment, and gaslighting pattern detection |
| AI–AI profile | Independent verification, unsupported consensus, error propagation, delegation scope, recovery | Proposed; application candidate is AI agent orchestration |
| Application interpretation | Reflection, relationship-risk review, orchestration feedback/control | Separate from descriptive canonical artifacts |

The same pattern need not mean the same thing in every profile. Strong delegation can be rational; disagreement can be performative. Human awareness and AI execution state are not interchangeable. Cross-profile comparisons require explicit correspondence and evidence, not reuse of an identical numeric scale by assumption.

The current raw input and canonical contracts remain Human–AI. This strategy does not add participant typing, graph storage, or new semantics to v1.5. Propose those changes in the upstream protocol before claiming canonical support.

## Automated Research and Distillation

Reference AI supplies independent evidence-backed labels and a separate challenge pass. Preserve dissent and an unknown/abstain result; do not equate consensus with truth. Random sampling complements uncertainty, disagreement, novelty, and boundary sampling so confidently wrong predictions remain discoverable. Treat same-model multiple passes as correlated evidence.

Maintain separate evaluation tracks:

- **Detector fidelity:** agreement with declared references, per-axis error, detection precision/recall, abstention and escalation coverage, latency, and total cost.
- **Framework validity:** counterexamples, style invariance, sensitivity to relevant behavioral changes, cross-domain transfer, and sampled external criteria/outcomes. A better imitation of the teacher does not by itself improve the construct.

Candidate axes may be revised or removed. Freeze a rubric within each experiment; any change produces a new framework version and a comparison against the previous version. No endless optimization on the same evaluation set.

## Applications and Product Sequence

1. Complete one automated loop on the existing Human–AI pipeline. Prove that successive iterations improve held-out performance or cost without degrading framework tests.
2. Prototype AI–AI as the first cross-profile experiment: link measurements to tool/test outcomes and compare orchestration policies with and without JDVP signals. This is a proposed first product experiment, not a demonstrated market advantage.
3. Explore Human–Human using participant-provided or otherwise authorized records. Surface evidence-backed patterns relevant to scams, coercive/cult recruitment, and gaslighting; a transcript pattern alone does not establish fraud, affiliation, intent, or a diagnosis. Keep missing context and uncertainty visible.
4. Offer a versioned measurement engine to research, education, and AI products if it adds value over direct reference-model analysis and simpler behavioral checklists.
5. Pursue conformance and licensing after independent implementation/replication and adoption. Independence can come from separate models, held-out domains, datasets, and reproducible execution; human expert sign-off is not a mandatory gate.

AI–AI control candidates include independent re-verification, new evidence retrieval, or return to the declared orchestration policy. Evaluate these in replay/sandbox experiments before enabling operational actions. Do not assume that increasing a JDVP score improves task outcomes.

## Evidence Gates

| Gate | Evidence required | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loop feasibility | Reproducible sample → reference/challenge → training → untouched evaluation, bounded spend, recoverable artifacts | Repeated automated research |
| Detector readiness | Predeclared per-profile quality/cost targets, input-specific uncertainty, coverage, comparison with direct reference calls and simple baselines | Low-cost measurement pilot |
| Framework validity | Counterexample and invariance tests, behavioral sensitivity, sampled external criteria, domain/profile transfer limits | Critical-thinking practice interpretation for the tested scope |
| Application value | Useful feedback or improved orchestration outcomes against a control, full operating cost, repeated use or purchase | Product expansion |
| Standard readiness | Versioned semantics, calibration procedures, independent replication, conformance tests and adoption | Standard/licensing claims |

Numerical promotion thresholds must be set in the first experiment plan before evaluating candidates; none is claimed achieved by this strategy. Human participation in supplying records or outcomes is distinct from human experts judging every sample.

## Differentiation and Boundaries

The proposed contribution is a transferable, temporal measurement framework coupled to an inexpensive implementation, not AI labeling or distillation alone. Test whether trajectories add useful information over single-turn scores and presence/absence checklists. Durable assets may include difficult counterexamples, cross-profile calibration, version history, and product integrations; these are hypotheses until demonstrated.

Use consent/authorization, data minimization, and access controls for human records. No employee surveillance, automated high-stakes ranking, or definitive accusations from conversational scores. Avoid claims about private cognition absent supporting evidence. Current synthetic and AI labels must retain their provenance and must never be relabeled as human annotations.
